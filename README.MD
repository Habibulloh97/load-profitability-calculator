#LoadWise

> Don't let the rate fool you. Book the right load with real profit math.

## The Problem

I drove a lease truck for over five years. All I ever got from dispatch was a Google Maps pin, the miles, and a rate. From there it was on me to figure out. Going back and forth with myself, whether there was actually decent profit left after my expenses. And the broker isn't waiting, dozens of other carriers are calling about the same load while you're still doing the math in your head.

Miss it, and you're not guaranteed another. I've sat dry at truck stops for days waiting on the next call. Other times I booked the wrong loads, ones that looked good but weren't, and my performance came in below what I knew I was capable of. Either way, it cost me and it cost the company.

The problem was never the driving. It was the decision: is this load actually worth it? A rate that looks good on paper can lose money once you factor deadhead miles, fuel, and tolls. I needed those numbers in seconds, not after the load was gone.

LoadWise is the tool I wish I'd had. Manage your fleet, drivers, and loads in one place, and when it's time to book, know you're making the right call for your operation.
